# Player account guide

This is what a player needs to know to use an email account in this game. It is not a developer document.

## Create an account

1. Open the game and choose Register.
2. Enter your email and a password of **15 to 128** characters. Avoid very common passwords.
3. Confirm the password. Accept the current Terms and Privacy checkboxes.
4. Submit. The game will not claim success until the account service accepts the request.
5. Check email for a verification code. Codes expire after a short time. If nothing arrives, wait, check junk, then use Resend.

You cannot play until the email is verified.

If registration fails with a generic “We could not create this account” message, the address may already be in use. Try Login or Forgot Password. The game will not tell you whether the address is already registered.

## Sign in

Use the same email and password. Wrong email or wrong password both show **Email or password is incorrect.**

If you see a wait timer, too many attempts were sent. Wait and try again. The account is not permanently locked.

Unverified accounts are sent to verification, not into the world.

## Sessions

- **Logout** ends this device’s session and returns to Login. Characters are kept.
- **Logout all devices** (Character Select, password confirm) ends every session. You will need to log in again everywhere.
- The game does not keep you signed in after close. Email sessions are not stored on disk.

## Password reset

From Login, choose Forgot Password. Enter the email. The success message is always the same, whether or not an account exists.

If you have an account, you receive a code. Enter it with a **new** password. You are **not** signed in automatically. Log in with the new password. The old password stops working. Other devices are signed out.

## Change password or email

On Character Select, open Account Settings.

- Change password: current password plus a new one. You return to Login.
- Change email: current password plus the new address. Confirm the code sent to the **new** address. Until you confirm, the old email still signs in. After confirm, only the new email works, and you must log in again.

Forgot which email you used? The in-game help does **not** look up or show an address. Contact support with your character names and approximate signup time.

## Characters

You may have **five** characters at a time. Classes are Warrior, Marksman, and Mage. Names must be unique (letter case does not create a new name).

Play a character from Character Select. Only one character per account can be in the world at a time.

Deleting a character requires typing its exact name. It stays restorable for **seven days** and still uses its name. Restoring it does not grant starter items again. After seven days it is removed and the name can be used by someone else.

## Disconnects

If the game closes or the network drops while you are in the world:

- Other players may still see your character for about **ten seconds** after the server notices the disconnect.
- That character cannot move or use abilities. Enemies can still hit it.
- You cannot play another character, and you cannot jump back in, until that hold ends.
- Character Select shows a countdown. After it finishes, Play works again. You will not see two copies of yourself.

Prefer **Quit Safely** (return to Character Select) or **Logout** when you can. Closing the window is not a logout.

## Export and delete the account

Account Settings can download a copy of your account data. The file is temporary; request a new export if it expires.

Deleting the account requires:

1. Your current password
2. A code sent to your email
3. Typing **DELETE ACCOUNT**
4. Confirming with the on-screen button (not Enter)

You cannot delete while a character is still in the world (including the ten-second hold), trading, or transferring. Return to Character Select first.

After deletion you cannot log in. The email can be used later to register a **new** empty account. Old characters, items, gold, and quests do not come back.

## Problems

- Account service unavailable: wait and retry, or try again later. Do not keep submitting.
- Email delayed: wait, check junk, resend. Local testers: mail is captured in the operator inbox, not Gmail.
- Client too old or too new: update the game.
